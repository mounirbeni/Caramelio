The Venue

A hidden gem in the heart of Targa

Caramelio is a spacious two-story café, bakery and restaurant offering cozy indoor seating alongside an open-air terrace. It's become a favorite among locals and visitors for its fast service, fair prices, and easy street parking — the kind of place that feels like a discovery.

Inside, an in-house boutique bakes fresh bread, traditional Moroccan pastries, savory appetizers and custom celebration cakes daily, while the kitchen serves breakfast, lunch and dinner featuring both Moroccan classics and international favorites.
