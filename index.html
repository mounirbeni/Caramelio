The Venue

A hidden gem in the heart of Targa

Caramelio is a spacious two-story café, bakery and restaurant offering cozy indoor seating alongside an open-air terrace — the kind of place that feels like a discovery. Fresh bread, pastries and celebration cakes are baked in-house daily, alongside a full breakfast, lunch and dinner menu.
